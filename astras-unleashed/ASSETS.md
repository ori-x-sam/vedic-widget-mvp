# Art prompts

The painted art Astras Unleashed needs, one prompt per file, for you to generate or commission. The game loads each
file the moment it exists and draws a placeholder until then (`public/art/README.md`). Paste a prompt into your image
tool together with the **shared style** block for its kind.

**Respect first (BRIEF.md §7):** every image must show the god as tradition does: the right arms, attributes, vahana
and skin colour; modest traditional attire; serene, warm faces (fierce forms are fierce in power, never cruel); no
blood, no violence shown, no sexualisation, no joke. Every iconography line marked **(verify)** is to be checked
against `LORE.md` and a practitioner before the art ships. Kali's depiction is to be reviewed by Shakta
practitioners.

## Shared style: card art (`public/art/cards/<id>.png`)

> Marvel Snap-style collectible card illustration. Portrait 5:7 (1000 × 1400 px). A single heroic figure filling the
> frame, shot from a dynamic low angle, with strong rim light, a glowing aura and a vivid background; the top of the head,
> crown or weapon touches the very top edge so it can break out of the card frame. Painterly digital illustration with
> rich, saturated, joyful colour in the palette of a 1970s–80s Indian children's textbook (sindoor #E8361F, haldi #FFC926,
> marigold #FF9414, rani pink #E62C7E, parrot green #38A845, peacock teal #00909A, indigo #26389A), deep contrast,
> clean readable silhouette. Keep the lower fifth of the image simpler and darker so a name can sit over it. No text,
> no lettering, no card frame, no border, no logos, no watermark. Not in the style of, and not resembling, any Marvel,
> Amar Chitra Katha, Clash Royale or other existing character.

## Shared style: arena figure (`public/art/units/<id>.png`)

> A game character for a Clash Royale-style mobile battler: chunky, heroic proportions (about 3 to 3.5 heads tall),
> bold readable silhouette, smooth soft shading like a polished 3D-rendered mobile game character, bright saturated
> colours, lit from the top left. Full body, standing, facing the camera at a slight 3/4 angle, viewed from slightly
> above. The signature attribute large and clearly visible. Transparent background, the feet touching the bottom edge,
> nothing cropped, about 512 × 640 px. No ground shadow (the game adds it), no text, no logos.

Code animates the figure (bounce, lunge, descent, lotus return), so one still pose per god is enough. If you later want
hand-drawn frames, a sprite sheet of idle, walk, strike can replace it.

## The arena (optional)

The arena is painted in code from the palette (grass, paths, river, bridges, towers, Kshetra scenery) and needs no
files. If you want painted textures later, these would slot in: a tileable grass texture (512 × 512, bright textbook
green #67BE3C with lighter #9BD957 tufts), a tileable river strip (#27AECF with #A6E9EE glints), a stone tower with a
bamboo staff and a Pipili-appliqué banner in the Sun's colours (#E8361F and #FFC926, a sun disc) and the Moon's
(#26389A and #F6EFE0, a crescent), each transparent, front 3/4 view from slightly above.
